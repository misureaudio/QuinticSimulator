## E. Method notes (for the essay's computational section)
  - Irreducibility over Q: f.is_irreducible() (mod-p + LLL).
  - Galois group: f.galois_group() (resolvent + mod-p Frobenius + GAP).
  - Frobenius cycle type at prime p: factor f mod p; the degrees of the
    irreducible factors are the cycle lengths of a Frobenius conjugate.
  - Discriminant square => G <= A_n.  A 3-cycle in a quintic with square
    discriminant forces G = A5 (C5, D5 have no 3-cycles).
  - Bad primes (p | disc) are skipped (repeated mod-p factors).
